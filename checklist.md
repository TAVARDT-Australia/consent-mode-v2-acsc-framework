# Australian Privacy Principles & Tag Governance Checklist

**Marco Regulatório:** Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) & ACSC Guidelines  
**Mantido por:** [TAVARDT Australia](https://ag.tavardt.com/au/)  

---

## Verificações Técnicas Obrigatórias

* **APP 1 & APP 5 Compliance:** Provide clear notice of collection before executing marketing pixels.
* **Default Consent Posture:** Ensure ad and analytics storage parameters default to 'denied' prior to user interaction.
* **PII Protection Audits:** Inspect telemetry payloads to prevent the transmission of identifiable user metadata.
* **Tag Sequencing in GTM:** Trigger analytics and remarketing strictly following verified consent update events.
