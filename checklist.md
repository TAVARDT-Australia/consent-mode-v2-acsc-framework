# Australian Privacy Principles & Consent Mode Checklist

**Regulatory Standard:** Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) & ACSC Guidelines  
**Maintained by:** [TAVARDT Australia](https://ag.tavardt.com/au/)  

---

## Technical Audit Points

* **Default Consent State:** Ensure all ad and analytics storage parameters default to 'denied' prior to user interaction.
* **APP 1 & APP 5 Compliance:** Provide clear notice of collection before executing marketing pixels.
* **Cross-Domain Linker:** Enable URL passthrough to preserve measurement without unauthorized client storage.
* **Tag Sequencing in GTM:** Configure triggers to fire analytics and remarketing only after explicit consent update events.
